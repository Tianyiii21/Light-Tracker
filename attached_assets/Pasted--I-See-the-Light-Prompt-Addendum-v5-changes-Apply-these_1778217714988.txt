## CHANGE 1 — BREATHING PAGE TITLE TEXT

The main text on Screen 1 must NEVER break mid-phrase due to screen width.

Replace the single sentence "The tide is here. So are you." with two
explicitly separate lines:

Line 1: "The tide is here."
Line 2: "So are you."

Each line should be its own HTML element (e.g. two separate spans or
paragraph elements, not one continuous string).

Styling:
- Both lines: Cormorant Garamond, warm gold, subtle glow
- Line 1: slightly larger (e.g. 26–28px)
- Line 2: slightly smaller and softer (e.g. 22–24px), as if a quiet reply
- Centered, with a small gap between the two lines
- Neither line should ever wrap — if needed, reduce font size slightly to
  ensure each fits on one line on a 375px wide screen (iPhone SE minimum)
- Test that both lines remain single-line at 375px viewport width

## CHANGE 2 — STARRY AUDIO ATMOSPHERE (replaces starry section from v4)

The starry atmosphere used on Screens 3 and 4 must feel light-hearted,
warm, and gently alive — not serene or overly still.

Replace the previous starry audio implementation with the following:

Generate using Web Audio API:

PLUCKED TONES:
- Use oscillators with a quick attack (0.01s) and medium release (1.2–1.8s)
  to simulate a soft plucked or chimed quality
- Play tones at frequencies from a pentatonic scale in a warm register:
  suggested frequencies: 523, 659, 784, 1047, 1319 Hz (C5 pentatonic)
- Trigger one tone every 1.5–3.5 seconds, chosen randomly from the scale
- Each tone: low gain (0.06–0.10), with a gentle reverb tail
- Vary the gain slightly per note for a natural, human feel
- The result should feel like distant wind chimes or a music box —
  light, warm, slightly playful, never mechanical

WARM PAD UNDERNEATH:
- Two oscillators slightly detuned (e.g. 261 Hz and 263 Hz) creating a
  gentle shimmer
- Very low gain (0.03–0.05), continuous
- Add reverb for spaciousness
- This sits underneath the plucked tones as a warm ambient foundation

DURING LANTERN RELEASE:
- Increase plucked tone frequency slightly (every 1–2 seconds instead of
  every 1.5–3.5) for a sense of gentle celebration
- Slightly increase pad gain for a soft swell
- Return to normal timing after the lantern settles (~6 seconds)

OVERALL FEELING:
Warm, wondering, gently celebratory — like watching lights rise into a
night sky with quiet joy. Not melancholic. Not silent. Alive but soft.

## CHANGE 3 — FULL SCREEN COVERAGE AND SAFE AREA (iOS fix)

The app must cover the entire phone screen including the status bar area.
There must be no black bars or color inconsistency at the top or bottom.

Apply ALL of the following:

HTML/META:
- Ensure viewport meta tag includes:
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1,
  viewport-fit=cover">
- Ensure manifest.json has:
  "display": "standalone"
  "background_color": "#0d1220"
  "theme_color": "#0d1220"

CSS:
- The root app container must use:
  min-height: 100dvh;  /* dynamic viewport height — handles iOS chrome */
  width: 100vw;
  background-color: #0d1220;  /* always fill with base dark color */
  padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top);
  padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom);

- All canvas elements must be sized to cover the full screen including
  safe areas. Use:
  position: absolute;
  top: 0; left: 0;
  width: 100%;
  height: 100%;
  and ensure the parent container has no gap at top or bottom.

- Set background-color: #0d1220 on both <html> and <body> elements so
  that even before the app renders, the screen is the correct dark color
  with no white or black flash.

CANVAS RESIZE:
- When resizing canvas on window resize, use:
  canvas.width = window.innerWidth;
  canvas.height = window.innerHeight;
  and immediately redraw the background gradient to prevent any flash.

- The sky gradient on Screen 1 must start at the very top of the canvas
  (y=0) with no gap. The darkest color (#0d1220) must reach the absolute
  top edge of the screen.

SCREEN TRANSITIONS:
- During cross-fade transitions between screens, the background color
  #0d1220 must always be visible underneath — never white or transparent.
  Use opacity transitions on screen content, not on the container itself.
